# Request: orders paid through a payment service or by foreign-currency invoices, shipped through a carrier, with refunds, retries and e-mail notices

An HTTP API for a shop that takes card payments through an outside payment service, ships its orders through an outside carrier, refunds parts of orders, issues invoices in foreign currencies, converted with an outside exchange-rate service, and turns events into e-mail notifications sent through an outside messaging service. Every request and response body is JSON. In a request body, a field written with `?` may be left out (its default is given); every other field named is required, and leaving it out is 400. Ids are strings the server assigns. Amounts are integers in yen. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings; a date that is not a real calendar date is 400.

- **The external services.** The payment service, the carrier, the exchange-rate service and the messaging service are all at the base URL in `process.env.SERVICE_URL`.
  - The payment service has two endpoints. Each request carries a JSON body and an `Idempotency-Key` header. `POST /charges` with `{"reference": orderId, "amount": yen, "token": string}` answers 201 with `{"id": string, "amount": integer}` when the card was charged, 402 with `{"declineCode": string}` when the card was declined, and 500 to 599 when it fails. `POST /refunds` with `{"charge": chargeId, "amount": yen, "reference": refundId}` answers 201 with `{"id": string, "amount": integer}` when the money was returned, 400 to 499 when it refuses, and 500 to 599 when it fails. Any answer not listed here (another status, or a body without those fields), or no answer, is a **failure**.
  - The carrier has two endpoints. `POST /shipments` with the JSON body `{"reference": orderId, "items": [{"sku", "qty"}, ...], "address": {"name", "postalCode", "line1"}}` and an `Idempotency-Key` header answers 201 with `{"id": string, "trackingNumber": string}` when it accepts the shipment, 400 to 499 when it rejects it, and 500 to 599 when it fails. `DELETE /shipments/{shipmentId}` (no body) answers 204 when the shipment is cancelled, 409 when the parcel was already picked up, and 500 to 599 when it fails. Any answer not listed here (for example a 200, or a 201 without `id` or `trackingNumber`), or no answer, is handled like a 5xx.
  - The exchange-rate service's only endpoint is `GET /rates/{currency}/JPY` (for example `GET /rates/USD/JPY`; no query or body). It answers 200 with `{"currency": string, "rate": number, "asOf": date}`: one unit of `currency` is worth `rate` yen as of `asOf`. A **valid answer** is a 200 whose body has these three fields, `currency` equal to the currency asked for, `rate` greater than 0 with at most 4 decimal places, and `asOf` a real date. Any other answer, or no answer, is a **failure**.
  - The messaging service's only endpoint is `POST /messages` with the JSON body `{"to": email, "subject": string, "text": string}` and an `Idempotency-Key` header. It answers 202 with `{"id": string}` when it accepts the message, 429 with a `Retry-After` header (a whole number of seconds) when the sender is sending too fast, 400 to 499 other than 429 when it rejects the message, and 500 to 599 when it fails. Any answer not listed above (for example a 200, or a 202 without `id`), or no answer, is handled like a 5xx.
- **The shop's date and the messaging time.** The server keeps a date, `today`, starting at `2026-11-02`, and the time `now`, a whole number of minutes starting at 0. `GET /clock` returns 200 with `{"today", "now"}`. `POST /clock` with `{"today": date}` moves the date forward (an earlier date is 409; the same date changes nothing and `retried` and `sent` are empty). When it moves to a later date, the app first makes the retries (below), then a delivery round (below) in which every `digest` user gets a digest, as if a multiple of 60 minutes had been passed; `now` does not change. It answers 200 with `{"today", "retried": [orderId, ...], "sent": [messageId, ...]}`: the orders for which it sent `POST /shipments` during this move, in the order sent, and the messages the messaging service accepted during this move, in the order sent.
- `POST /clock/minutes` with `{"advance": integer}` (1 to 1440) adds to `now` and then makes a delivery round; 200 with `{"now", "sent": [messageId, ...]}`, the messages the service accepted during the round, in the order sent.
- `POST /products` with `{"sku": string, "stock": integer}` adds a product (sku 1 to 40 characters and unique, 409 if taken; stock 0 to 10,000). 201 with `{"sku", "stock", "reserved": 0, "available"}`, where `available` is `stock - reserved`. `POST /products/{sku}/restock` with `{"qty"}` (1 to 10,000) adds to `stock`; 200 with the product. `GET /products/{sku}` returns 200 with the product.
- `POST /orders` with `{"lines": [{"sku": string, "unitPrice": integer, "qty": integer}, ...], "discount"?: integer, "address": {"name", "postalCode", "line1"}, "userId"?: string}` creates an unpaid order: 1 to 10 lines with distinct skus of existing products (an unknown sku is 404), `unitPrice` 1 to 1,000,000, `qty` 1 to 100, `discount` from 0 (the default) to less than the subtotal, address strings 1 to 100 characters, `userId` (default null) a user who has not left (404 if unknown, 409 if they left). Nothing is reserved or sent. 201 with the order `{"id", "status": "unpaid", "lines", "subtotal", "discount", "total", "chargeId": null, "attempts": 0, "declines": [], "refunded": 0, "refunds": [], "address", "shipStatus": "new", "confirmations": 0, "shipAttempts": 0, "shipmentId": null, "trackingNumber": null, "holdReason": null, "invoiceId": null, "userId"}`. Each line is `{"sku", "unitPrice", "qty", "net", "refundedQty": 0, "refundedAmount": 0}`. `status` is the payment status and `shipStatus` the shipping status.
- **Discount.** A line's subtotal is `unitPrice * qty`; `subtotal` is their sum and `total` is `subtotal - discount`. The discount is split over the lines in proportion to their subtotals: each line gets `floor(discount * lineSubtotal / subtotal)`, and the yen left over go one each to the lines with the largest fractional parts of `discount * lineSubtotal / subtotal` (ties: the earlier line). A line's `net` is its subtotal minus its share; the nets add up to `total`.
- **Paying.** `POST /orders/{id}/pay` with `{"token": string}` (1 to 200 characters) is allowed for an `unpaid` order whose `invoiceId` is null (409 otherwise; see invoicing an order). It adds 1 to `attempts` and sends `POST /charges` with the order's id, its `total` and the token, and the header `Idempotency-Key: charge-{orderId}-{attempts}`. Then:
  - 201 with `amount` equal to `total`: the order is `paid` and `chargeId` is the answer's `id`; 200 with the order;
  - 201 with another `amount`: refused with 502, and the order stays unpaid;
  - 402: refused with 402 and `{"error": string, "declineCode"}`; the code is appended to `declines`. After the third decline the order is `blocked`: it can no longer be paid;
  - a failure: refused with 503, and the order stays unpaid.
  Only `attempts`, `declines` and `status` change on a refused payment.
- **Open units.** A line's open units are `qty - refundedQty`: the units still to ship. Shipping below always uses them, never `qty`.
- **Confirming.** `POST /orders/{id}/confirm` is allowed when `shipStatus` is `new` or `on_hold` and `status` is `paid` or `partially_refunded` (409 otherwise). It adds 1 to `confirmations`, sets `shipAttempts` to 0 and makes an attempt. 200 with the order after the attempt, whatever the carrier answered.
- **An attempt.** If any line's open units are more than its product's `available`, the order goes `on_hold` with `holdReason` `"stock"`, and nothing is sent. Otherwise `shipAttempts` grows by 1 and the app sends `POST /shipments` with `reference` the order's id, `items` the lines that have open units, in their order, each with `qty` its open units, the order's address, and the header `Idempotency-Key: {orderId}-{confirmations}`. Every attempt of one confirmation therefore sends the same key, and confirming again after a hold sends a new one. Then:
  - on 201 `shipStatus` is `booked`, with `shipmentId` (the answer's `id`) and `trackingNumber`; only now are the open units added to their products' `reserved`;
  - on 400 to 499 it goes `on_hold` with `holdReason` `"carrier"`;
  - otherwise it is `retrying`, or `failed` when `shipAttempts` has reached 3. A failed order is final for shipping.
  `holdReason` is null in every `shipStatus` but `on_hold`.
- **Retries.** When the clock moves to a later date, every `retrying` order makes one attempt, in the order the orders were created. A retry checks stock first like any attempt, so it may put the order on hold without sending anything, and stock reserved by an earlier retry in the same move counts.
- **Cancelling shipping.** `POST /orders/{id}/cancel`: an order whose `shipStatus` is `new`, `on_hold` or `retrying` becomes `cancelled` at once and nothing is sent (a retrying order is not retried again). For a `booked` order the app sends `DELETE /shipments/{shipmentId}`: on 204 it is `cancelled` and its open units leave `reserved`; on 409 the cancel is refused with 409 and the order stays `booked`; on anything else it is refused with 503 and the order stays `booked`. Any other `shipStatus` is 409 and nothing is sent. 200 with the order. Cancelling does not refund.
- `POST /orders/{id}/handover` records that the carrier collected a `booked` order: it becomes `shipped`, and its open units leave both `stock` and `reserved`. 409 for any other `shipStatus`. Nothing is sent.
- **Refunding.** `POST /orders/{id}/refunds` with `{"lines": [{"line": index, "qty"}, ...]}` refunds units of a `paid` or `partially_refunded` order that was paid by card and whose `shipStatus` is not `retrying` or `booked` (409 otherwise). `line` is a 0-based line index (400 if there is no such line), listed at most once, and `qty` is 1 to that line's `qty - refundedQty` (409 if more). Each listed line's amount is `floor(net * q / qty)` for `q` of the line's `qty` units, except when the refund takes the line's last remaining units: then it is `net - refundedAmount`. The refund's `amount` is the sum. The app creates the refund with an id and sends `POST /refunds` with the order's `chargeId`, the amount and the refund's id, and the header `Idempotency-Key: refund-{refundId}`.
  - 201 with `amount` equal to the refund's amount: the refund `succeeded`. Each line's `refundedQty` and `refundedAmount` grow, the order's `refunded` grows by the amount, and the order becomes `refunded` when every unit of every line is refunded, else `partially_refunded`. If it becomes `refunded` while `shipStatus` is `new` or `on_hold`, `shipStatus` becomes `cancelled` (nothing is sent to the carrier). 201 with the refund.
  - Anything else: the refund is `failed` and nothing else changes; 502 with `{"error": string, "refundId"}`.
  A refund is `{"id", "amount", "status", "lines": [{"line", "qty", "amount"}, ...], "attempts"}` and is appended to the order's `refunds` in either case; `attempts` counts the requests sent for it.
- **Retrying a refund.** `POST /refunds/{id}/retry` sends a `failed` refund again (409 for a refund that succeeded, and 409 while the order's `shipStatus` is `retrying` or `booked`). Its lines must still fit the remaining units (409 otherwise); the amounts are not recomputed. It is sent with the same `reference` and the same `Idempotency-Key` as before, and the answer is handled as above (201 with the refund on success, 502 on failure).
- `DELETE /orders/{id}` deletes a `blocked` order or an `unpaid` one whose `invoiceId` is null (409 otherwise). Nothing is sent. 204.
- `GET /orders/{id}` returns 200 with the order. `GET /orders?shipStatus=...` returns 200 with the orders in creation order, only those with that `shipStatus` when given (one of `new`, `on_hold`, `retrying`, `booked`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `shipped`; another value is 400). `GET /refunds/{id}` returns 200 with the refund plus its `orderId`.
- **Getting a rate.** When a rule below needs today's rate of a currency, the app uses the rate it kept for that currency and today, if it has one. Otherwise it sends one `GET /rates/{currency}/JPY`:
  - a valid answer whose `asOf` is the day before today or later is **fresh**: the app keeps that rate for the currency and today, and uses it;
  - a valid answer with an earlier `asOf` is **stale**: the operation is refused with 502 and nothing is kept;
  - a failure refuses the operation with 503 and nothing is kept.
  A kept rate is used only on the date it was kept for; after a stale answer or a failure the app asks again next time.
- The app asks for a rate only after every other check of the request passed: a request refused with 400, 404 or 409 sends nothing to the service. A refused invoice operation (400, 404, 409, 502, 503) changes nothing.
- **Converting.** `yen(cents, rate)` is `cents * rate / 100` rounded down to an integer, computed exactly (1999 cents at a rate of 151.25 is 3023 yen). Foreign amounts are integers in cents (1/100 of the currency).
- `POST /invoices` with `{"customer": string, "currency": "USD" | "EUR" | "GBP", "lines": [{"description": string, "amount": cents}, ...], "orderId"?: string}` creates a draft: customer and description 1 to 100 characters, 1 to 20 lines, each amount 1 to 10,000,000. 201 with the invoice `{"id", "number": null, "status": "draft", "orderId", "customer", "currency", "lines", "total", "rate": null, "rateDate": null, "totalYen": null, "paid": 0, "paidYen": 0, "balanceYen": null, "fxDiffYen": null, "payments": []}`; `total` is the sum of the line amounts.
- **Invoicing an order.** With `orderId` (null when not given) the invoice bills that order (404 if unknown). The order must be `unpaid` and have no other invoice that is not `void` (409 otherwise). The order's `invoiceId` is its invoice that is not `void`, or null. The invoice's currency and lines are not checked against the order. When the invoice becomes `paid`, the order becomes `paid` with `chargeId` null: it can then be confirmed and shipped like a card-paid order, but not refunded. Voiding the invoice sets the order's `invoiceId` back to null.
- `PUT /invoices/{id}/lines` with `{"lines": [...]}` replaces the lines of a draft (same rules as creating; 409 when the invoice is not a draft) and recomputes `total`. 200 with the invoice.
- `POST /invoices/{id}/issue` issues a draft (409 for any other status). It gets today's rate of the invoice's currency. On success the invoice gets `status` `issued`, `rate`, `rateDate` = today, `totalYen` = `yen(total, rate)`, `balanceYen` = `totalYen`, and the next number. Numbers are `INV-0001`, `INV-0002`, ... in the order invoices were issued; a refused issue takes no number and leaves the invoice a draft. 200 with the invoice.
- `POST /invoices/{id}/payments` with `{"amount": cents}` records a payment in the invoice's currency on today's date. 409 unless the invoice is `issued` or `partly_paid`; `amount` is 1 to `total - paid` (409 if more). The payment is converted at today's rate, gotten as above (on the issue date, the issue's kept rate): its `yen` is `yen(amount, rate)`. 201 with the payment `{"id", "amount", "date", "rate", "yen"}`, appended to the invoice's `payments`. `paid` and `paidYen` are the sums of the payments' `amount` and `yen`.
- **Status and balance after a payment.** While `paid` is below `total`, the status is `partly_paid` and `balanceYen` is `totalYen - paidYen` (negative when the rate rose). When `paid` reaches `total`, the status is `paid`, `balanceYen` is 0 and `fxDiffYen` is `paidYen - totalYen` (the exchange gain or loss); before that, `fxDiffYen` is null.
- `POST /invoices/{id}/void` voids an `issued` invoice that has no payments (409 for any other status, including a draft). Its status becomes `void` and `balanceYen` 0; it keeps its number, and the number is never given to another invoice. 200 with the invoice.
- `GET /invoices/{id}` returns 200 with the invoice. `GET /invoices?status=...` returns 200 with the invoices in creation order, only those with that status when `status` is given (a status that is not one of `draft`, `issued`, `partly_paid`, `paid`, `void` is 400).
- `GET /reports/fx?from=date&to=date` returns 200 with `{"gainYen", "lossYen", "invoices": [number, ...]}` over the invoices paid in full whose last payment is dated from `from` to `to` (inclusive, both required): `gainYen` is the sum of their positive `fxDiffYen`, `lossYen` the sum of their negative ones as a positive number, and `invoices` lists their numbers in issue order.
- `POST /users` with `{"email": string, "mode": "instant" | "digest", "muted"?: [topic, ...]}` adds a user: `email` 3 to 200 characters with exactly one `@`, `muted` a list of distinct topics (default empty). 201 with the user `{"id", "email", "mode", "muted", "pending": 0, "pausedUntil": null, "left": false}`. `pending` counts the user's events waiting for a digest.
- `PUT /users/{id}` with `{"mode", "muted"}` changes a user's settings (200 with the user). When a `digest` user becomes `instant`, their pending events are made into a digest message at once (below), and the user's queue is sent.
- `DELETE /users/{id}` makes the user leave: `left` becomes true, their pending events are dropped and every `queued` message of theirs becomes `discarded`. Nothing more is ever sent to them. 204. Events, settings changes and a second leave for a user who left are 409.
- **Events.** `POST /events` with `{"userId", "topic": string, "text": string}` (topic 1 to 40 characters, text 1 to 500) records an event for a user. 201 with `{"id", "outcome", "messageId"}`:
  - if the topic is in the user's `muted`, `outcome` is `"muted"` and nothing else happens;
  - for a `digest` user the event waits for the next digest: `outcome` `"pending"`, and `pending` grows by 1;
  - for an `instant` user a message with `subject` the topic and `text` the event's text is added at the end of the user's queue (`outcome` `"message"`), and the user's queue is sent.
  `messageId` is the id of the message the event made, or null.
- **Notices of orders.** For an order with a `userId`, the app records an event for that user, as `POST /events` does, right after each of these changes: the carrier books the order (topic `shipment`, text `Order {orderId} booked: {trackingNumber}`); a refund of the order succeeds (topic `refund`, text `Refund {refundId}: {amount} yen`); the order becomes `paid` through its invoice (topic `invoice`, text `Order {orderId} paid by {number}`). A message such an event makes is sent before the request answers. Nothing is recorded for a user who has left. The messaging service's answers never change the order, the refund or the invoice, nor the answer to the request.
- **Messages.** A message is `{"id", "userId", "subject", "text", "status", "attempts", "serviceId"}`; `status` is `queued`, `sent`, `failed` or `discarded`, `attempts` counts the requests sent for it and `serviceId` is the service's `id` once accepted (null before). A user's queue is their `queued` messages in creation order.
- **Sending a user's queue.** Nothing is sent while the user is paused (`pausedUntil` is greater than `now`). Otherwise the app sends the queued messages one at a time, oldest first, each as `POST /messages` with the user's email, the message's subject and text, and the header `Idempotency-Key: {messageId}` (the same key every time the message is sent). For each answer:
  - 202: the message is `sent` and the next one is sent;
  - 429: the message stays queued, `pausedUntil` becomes `now` plus the `Retry-After` seconds rounded up to whole minutes (at least 1; a missing or non-integer `Retry-After` counts as 60 seconds), and sending stops for this user;
  - other 400 to 499: the message is `failed` and the next one is sent;
  - anything else: the message stays queued and sending stops for this user until something sends their queue again.
  Other users are not affected.
- **Digests.** A digest is one message made from all of a user's pending events, oldest first: `subject` `"Digest: {n} updates"` (`"Digest: 1 update"` for one event) and `text` the events' texts joined with a newline (`"\n"`). It joins the end of the user's queue and `pending` returns to 0. With no pending events no digest is made.
- **The delivery round.** For each user who has not left, in the order the users were added: if `pausedUntil` is not greater than `now`, it becomes null; if the user is in `digest` mode and the clock move reached or passed a multiple of 60 minutes (60, 120, ...), a digest is made (one per round, however many multiples were passed); then the user's queue is sent.
- `GET /users/{id}` returns 200 with the user. `GET /users/{id}/messages` returns 200 with all of the user's messages in creation order. `GET /messages/{id}` returns 200 with a message.
- The app sends to a service only after every other check of the request passed: a request refused with 400 or 404, or with 409 by the app's own checks, sends nothing and changes nothing.
- Errors: a body that is not valid JSON, a missing or wrong-typed field, or a value outside the rules above is 400; an unknown order, product, refund, invoice, user or message is 404; the conflicts named above are 409. Every error body (502 and 503 too) is `{"error": string}`, with the extra fields named above.
- Data is kept in memory; it does not have to survive a restart.
