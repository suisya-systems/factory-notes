# Request: orders paid through a payment service and shipped through a carrier, with refunds and retries

An HTTP API for a shop that takes card payments through an outside payment service, ships its orders through an outside carrier, and refunds parts of orders. Every request and response body is JSON. Ids are strings the server assigns. Amounts are integers in yen. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings; a date that is not a real calendar date is 400.

- **The external services.** The payment service and the carrier are both at the base URL in `process.env.SERVICE_URL`.
  - The payment service has two endpoints. Each request carries a JSON body and an `Idempotency-Key` header. `POST /charges` with `{"reference": orderId, "amount": yen, "token": string}` answers 201 with `{"id": string, "amount": integer}` when the card was charged, 402 with `{"declineCode": string}` when the card was declined, and 500 to 599 when it fails. `POST /refunds` with `{"charge": chargeId, "amount": yen, "reference": refundId}` answers 201 with `{"id": string, "amount": integer}` when the money was returned, 400 to 499 when it refuses, and 500 to 599 when it fails. Any answer not listed here (another status, or a body without those fields), or no answer, is a **failure**.
  - The carrier has two endpoints. `POST /shipments` with the JSON body `{"reference": orderId, "items": [{"sku", "qty"}, ...], "address": {"name", "postalCode", "line1"}}` and an `Idempotency-Key` header answers 201 with `{"id": string, "trackingNumber": string}` when it accepts the shipment, 400 to 499 when it rejects it, and 500 to 599 when it fails. `DELETE /shipments/{shipmentId}` (no body) answers 204 when the shipment is cancelled, 409 when the parcel was already picked up, and 500 to 599 when it fails. Any answer not listed here (for example a 200, or a 201 without `id` or `trackingNumber`), or no answer, is handled like a 5xx.
- **The shop's date.** The server keeps a date, `today`, starting at `2026-11-02`. `GET /clock` returns 200 with `{"today"}`. `POST /clock` with `{"today": date}` moves it forward (an earlier date is 409; the same date changes nothing and `retried` is empty). When it moves to a later date, the app first makes the retries (below), then answers 200 with `{"today", "retried": [orderId, ...]}`, the orders for which it sent `POST /shipments` during this move, in the order sent.
- `POST /products` with `{"sku": string, "stock": integer}` adds a product (sku 1 to 40 characters and unique, 409 if taken; stock 0 to 10,000). 201 with `{"sku", "stock", "reserved": 0, "available"}`, where `available` is `stock - reserved`. `POST /products/{sku}/restock` with `{"qty"}` (1 to 10,000) adds to `stock`; 200 with the product. `GET /products/{sku}` returns 200 with the product.
- `POST /orders` with `{"lines": [{"sku": string, "unitPrice": integer, "qty": integer}, ...], "discount"?: integer, "address": {"name", "postalCode", "line1"}}` creates an unpaid order: 1 to 10 lines with distinct skus of existing products (an unknown sku is 404), `unitPrice` 1 to 1,000,000, `qty` 1 to 100, `discount` from 0 (the default) to less than the subtotal, address strings 1 to 100 characters. Nothing is reserved or sent. 201 with the order `{"id", "status": "unpaid", "lines", "subtotal", "discount", "total", "chargeId": null, "attempts": 0, "declines": [], "refunded": 0, "refunds": [], "address", "shipStatus": "new", "confirmations": 0, "shipAttempts": 0, "shipmentId": null, "trackingNumber": null, "holdReason": null}`. Each line is `{"sku", "unitPrice", "qty", "net", "refundedQty": 0, "refundedAmount": 0}`. `status` is the payment status and `shipStatus` the shipping status.
- **Discount.** A line's subtotal is `unitPrice * qty`; `subtotal` is their sum and `total` is `subtotal - discount`. The discount is split over the lines in proportion to their subtotals: each line gets `floor(discount * lineSubtotal / subtotal)`, and the yen left over go one each to the lines with the largest fractional parts of `discount * lineSubtotal / subtotal` (ties: the earlier line). A line's `net` is its subtotal minus its share; the nets add up to `total`.
- **Paying.** `POST /orders/{id}/pay` with `{"token": string}` (1 to 200 characters) is allowed for an `unpaid` order (409 otherwise). It adds 1 to `attempts` and sends `POST /charges` with the order's id, its `total` and the token, and the header `Idempotency-Key: charge-{orderId}-{attempts}`. Then:
  - 201 with `amount` equal to `total`: the order is `paid` and `chargeId` is the answer's `id`; 200 with the order;
  - 201 with another `amount`: refused with 502, and the order stays unpaid;
  - 402: refused with 402 and `{"error": string, "declineCode"}`; the code is appended to `declines`. After the third decline the order is `blocked`: it can no longer be paid;
  - a failure: refused with 503, and the order stays unpaid.
  Only `attempts`, `declines` and `status` change on a refused payment.
- **Open units.** A line's open units are `qty - refundedQty`: the units still to ship. Shipping below always uses them, never `qty`.
- **Confirming.** `POST /orders/{id}/confirm` is allowed when `shipStatus` is `new` or `on_hold` and `status` is `paid` or `partially_refunded` (409 otherwise). It adds 1 to `confirmations`, sets `shipAttempts` to 0 and makes an attempt. 200 with the order after the attempt, whatever the carrier answered.
- **An attempt.** If any line's open units are more than its product's `available`, the order goes `on_hold` with `holdReason` `"stock"`, and nothing is sent. Otherwise `shipAttempts` grows by 1 and the app sends `POST /shipments` with `reference` the order's id, `items` the lines that have open units, in their order, each with `qty` its open units, the order's address, and the header `Idempotency-Key: {orderId}-{confirmations}`. Every attempt of one confirmation therefore sends the same key, and confirming again after a hold sends a new one. Then:
  - on 201 `shipStatus` is `booked`, with `shipmentId` (the answer's `id`) and `trackingNumber`; only now are the open units added to their products' `reserved`;
  - on 400 to 499 it goes `on_hold` with `holdReason` `"carrier"`;
  - otherwise it is `retrying`, or `failed` when `shipAttempts` has reached 3. A failed order is final for shipping.
  `holdReason` is null in every `shipStatus` but `on_hold`.
- **Retries.** When the clock moves to a later date, every `retrying` order makes one attempt, in the order the orders were created. A retry checks stock first like any attempt, so it may put the order on hold without sending anything, and stock reserved by an earlier retry in the same move counts.
- **Cancelling shipping.** `POST /orders/{id}/cancel`: an order whose `shipStatus` is `new`, `on_hold` or `retrying` becomes `cancelled` at once and nothing is sent (a retrying order is not retried again). For a `booked` order the app sends `DELETE /shipments/{shipmentId}`: on 204 it is `cancelled` and its open units leave `reserved`; on 409 the cancel is refused with 409 and the order stays `booked`; on anything else it is refused with 503 and the order stays `booked`. Any other `shipStatus` is 409 and nothing is sent. 200 with the order. Cancelling does not refund.
- `POST /orders/{id}/handover` records that the carrier collected a `booked` order: it becomes `shipped`, and its open units leave both `stock` and `reserved`. 409 for any other `shipStatus`. Nothing is sent.
- **Refunding.** `POST /orders/{id}/refunds` with `{"lines": [{"line": index, "qty"}, ...]}` refunds units of a `paid` or `partially_refunded` order whose `shipStatus` is not `retrying` or `booked` (409 otherwise). `line` is a 0-based line index (400 if there is no such line), listed at most once, and `qty` is 1 to that line's `qty - refundedQty` (409 if more). Each listed line's amount is `floor(net * q / qty)` for `q` of the line's `qty` units, except when the refund takes the line's last remaining units: then it is `net - refundedAmount`. The refund's `amount` is the sum. The app creates the refund with an id and sends `POST /refunds` with the order's `chargeId`, the amount and the refund's id, and the header `Idempotency-Key: refund-{refundId}`.
  - 201 with `amount` equal to the refund's amount: the refund `succeeded`. Each line's `refundedQty` and `refundedAmount` grow, the order's `refunded` grows by the amount, and the order becomes `refunded` when every unit of every line is refunded, else `partially_refunded`. If it becomes `refunded` while `shipStatus` is `new` or `on_hold`, `shipStatus` becomes `cancelled` (nothing is sent to the carrier). 201 with the refund.
  - Anything else: the refund is `failed` and nothing else changes; 502 with `{"error": string, "refundId"}`.
  A refund is `{"id", "amount", "status", "lines": [{"line", "qty", "amount"}, ...], "attempts"}` and is appended to the order's `refunds` in either case; `attempts` counts the requests sent for it.
- **Retrying a refund.** `POST /refunds/{id}/retry` sends a `failed` refund again (409 for a refund that succeeded, and 409 while the order's `shipStatus` is `retrying` or `booked`). Its lines must still fit the remaining units (409 otherwise); the amounts are not recomputed. It is sent with the same `reference` and the same `Idempotency-Key` as before, and the answer is handled as above (201 with the refund on success, 502 on failure).
- `DELETE /orders/{id}` deletes an `unpaid` or `blocked` order (409 otherwise). Nothing is sent. 204.
- `GET /orders/{id}` returns 200 with the order. `GET /orders?shipStatus=...` returns 200 with the orders in creation order, only those with that `shipStatus` when given (one of `new`, `on_hold`, `retrying`, `booked`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `shipped`; another value is 400). `GET /refunds/{id}` returns 200 with the refund plus its `orderId`.
- The app sends to a service only after every other check of the request passed: a request refused with 400 or 404, or with 409 by the app's own checks, sends nothing and changes nothing.
- Errors: a body that is not valid JSON, a missing or wrong-typed field, or a value outside the rules above is 400; an unknown order, product or refund is 404; the conflicts named above are 409. Every error body is `{"error": string}`, with the extra fields named above.
- Data is kept in memory; it does not have to survive a restart.
